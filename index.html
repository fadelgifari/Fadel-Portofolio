Featured project

JoRUN AI · Your Fisherman Friend

Journey of Optimal Recovery and Unlocking New Potential. An AI decision-support system for fishing operations, the jobs that recover tools and equipment stuck downhole.

The problem

Fishing jobs in Zona Rokan relied on individual experience and service-company recommendations. Records were manual and scattered across Excel, so the same mistakes repeated and failed jobs cost rig time and production.

What I built with the team

  • FOCUS (Fishing Optimization & Candidate Underpinning Strategy) digitized the fishing workflow and ranks candidate wells and tool strings.
  • A Random Forest model trained on labeled Zona Rokan well history predicts job success, chosen over XGBoost, KNN and Bayesian models.
  • A RAG advisor searches historical reports with BM25 and answers in English or Bahasa through Qwen3-32B running on-premise on Dataiku LLM Mesh.
  • Engineers can read impression-block photos, preview the BHA and job steps before running them, and generate a full fishing program.
  • Data from Oracle WellView and the Workover Daily Report flows into Dataiku, so status is near real-time across 7,258 fishing events.

P.R.O.T.E.C.T · Digital rig safeguard checks

A digital Comprehensive Rig Safeguard System Check (CRSSC) that replaces paper checklists, so safety is verified by the crew at the wellsite with photo evidence, not on paper after the fact.

What it does

  • Offline-first mobile app on PHR OneApp: role-based checklists for 7 field roles, with data kept on the phone and synced automatically when signal returns.
  • Photo evidence with watermarks stamping rig, user, role, GPS and time on every photo, so checks cannot be ticked without proof.
  • Stop-Work Authority flow: equipment marked Not Ready triggers notification and approval before work continues.
  • Web dashboard for real-time rig status, compliance by role, an evidence gallery and one-click PDF reports.

My part

Part of the PROTECT Squad, a collaboration of WOWI and IT. I framed the problem, led UAT, go-live, socialization and field implementation across rigs, tracked value creation, and shaped the PROTECT 2.0 AI roadmap.

Featured project · Beta Oct 2026

QSERRA · PJSM Anti Lupa

Quality Supervision on Reliable Rig Activity. Digitizes pre-job safety meetings (PJSM) and work supervision for every DWI rig, so nothing gets skipped: the 3 pillars (WSR, FHL, Toolpusher) fill four locked-in-order forms on their phones, and the answers become a live dashboard.

How it works

  • One Job ID, four forms: Pre-Work Supervision, PJSM, Execution and Debrief, unlocked in order (A → B → C → D) so no step can be skipped.
  • Anti-lupa rules: mandatory safety checklists, at least one photo per form, a reason required for every "No", and submitted data that cannot be deleted from the field.
  • Works offline on QSERRA Mobile (Power Apps) and syncs automatically; job codes, personnel and 16,000+ wells searchable by typing.
  • Job guides with 3D videos for 61 job types (414 steps): work steps, hazards, PPE and PJSM discussion points, played with the crew during the meeting.
  • Six-tab dashboard: readiness, PJSM quality, execution and 3-pillar supervision, crew/shift performance mapping, plan vs actual time, and safety findings with evidence.

My part

Co-authored the business requirements (Forms A to D, 77 field specs, 11 business rules), built the mobile and desktop apps on Power Apps, SharePoint and Power Automate, produced the 3D job animations and the user guide, and launched the Beta in October 2026.

Featured project · Beta Oct 2026

SOP L.E.G.O. Hub

The PHR SOP file hub for Drilling & Well Intervention. Contractors send rig SOPs by email, the system checks and routes them to PHR acknowledgers in Teams and Outlook, and every decision is kept as an audit trail.

What it does

  • Structured email intake: contractors submit with a fixed subject and body template (rig, SOP number, revision), so every submission is read and filed automatically.
  • Area Manager approval check: each submission must carry an "Approved by Area Manager" line. If it is missing, the email is rejected automatically and the sender is told why.
  • One-tap acknowledgement in Teams: PHR acknowledgers approve or reject from an approval card that shows the SOP and the approving Area Manager.
  • Revision snips: when rejecting, acknowledgers can attach screenshots of the exact SOP sections to fix (from PC or phone). They are emailed to the contractor and stored with the submission.

Why it matters

Contractor leadership now owns the content of every SOP before it reaches PHR, only internally reviewed SOPs enter the review queue, and contractors see exactly what to fix, so revision cycles get shorter.

My part

Built the intake, approval and revision-snip flows on Power Automate, SharePoint, Teams and Outlook, and act as the contact for questions and access.
